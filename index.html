How it works

From paper receipt to ready-to-share record in three steps.

  1. 1

    Capture and check

    Scan with the camera or import from Photos, then review and correct the extracted details.

  2. 2

    Give it context

    Pick a profile and category, mark it business or personal, and add notes or warranty dates.

  3. 3

    Prepare and share

    Use Export Receipts to filter, review amounts and choose a file format.

Features

Everything a receipt needs, nothing it doesn't.

Six parts that work together, from the moment you scan to the file you hand to your accountant.

01 / CAPTURE

Scan in seconds

On-device OCR extracts the details, and you review them before anything is saved.

02 / ORGANISE

Profiles, categories, search

Separate profiles, categories and business/personal tags. Search across trading names, legal entities, notes and receipt numbers.

03 / WORKSPACE

Pick up where you left off

Home returns to your last profile, with quick access to Receipts, Scan, Finance and More.

04 / WARRANTY

Never miss an expiry

Record expiry dates, filter them — archived receipts included — and turn on optional local reminders per profile.

05 / REVIEW

Catch gaps before export

Filters and missing-information checks. Totals stay separate by currency — no automatic conversion.

06 / EXPORT

CSV and PDF, your choice

CSV records, a financial summary PDF, or a receipt PDF archive with images — with an optional archive prompt after sharing.

Good to know

Tax figures are estimates

Calculated with a configured single, tax-inclusive rate and currency-level rounding. Check against original receipts, especially with mixed rates or exempt items.

Not a filing tool

The app does not file tax returns or certify accounting compliance.

Warranty dates are your records

They are not a guarantee of coverage.

Exports aren't backups

CSV and PDF files are not a complete, restorable backup of the app.
