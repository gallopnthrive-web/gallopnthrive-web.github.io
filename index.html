Handheld Accountant · iPhone and iPad

Your receipts.Under control.

Scan, organise and review your receipts. Keep warranty dates close, and prepare useful records for yourself or your accountant—without creating an app account.

View current App Store version Explore 2.0 features

Receipt text recognition and storage happen on your device. Advertising and purchase services can use the network; see our Privacy Policy.

From a receipt to a useful record.

  1. Capture and check

    Scan with the camera or import an image from Photos. Review the detected details and correct anything that is missing or inaccurate.

  2. Give it context

    Choose a profile and category, mark Business or Personal, and add notes or a warranty expiry date where useful.

  3. Prepare and share

    Open Export Receipts, filter your selection, review the amounts, and choose the file that suits your next step.

More clarity.
Less receipt admin.

Tools for personal records, projects and small businesses. You stay in control of the details and what gets shared.

01 / CAPTURE

Scan, then review

On-device OCR helps extract receipt details. Check the merchant, date and total, and enter or correct other fields before saving.

02 / ORGANISE

Keep contexts separate

Switch profiles, categorise receipts, and distinguish business from personal expenses. Search trading names, legal entities, notes and receipt numbers.

03 / WORKSPACE

Pick up where you left off

Return to Home for your last selected profile. Receipts, Scan, Finance and More keep the main tools within reach.

04 / WARRANTY

Keep dates in view

Record warranty expiry dates and filter warranty records, including archived receipts. Optional local reminders are controlled separately for each profile.

05 / REVIEW

Check before exporting

Filter records and review missing information before sharing. Totals stay separated by currency—without automatic conversion or mixed-currency sums.

06 / EXPORT

Choose a useful format

Create CSV records, a financial summary PDF, or a receipt PDF archive with images. Sharing a receipt PDF can be followed by a separate archiving confirmation.

Preparation and review are available without Full Version. CSV/PDF file creation, Spending Summary, custom category management and historical category suggestions require Full Version. Full Version is a one-time purchase, with no subscription. Prices are shown in the app for your App Store region.

An assistant for your records.

Tax figures use the configured single-rate, tax-inclusive estimate and currency-level rounding. Check them against the original receipt, especially for mixed rates or exempt items. Handheld Accountant does not file tax returns or certify accounting compliance.

Warranty dates are your records, not a guarantee of coverage. CSV and PDF files preserve exported information but are not a complete, restorable backup of the app.
